# Shared brief — AstraZeneca Equal Care Across Finland
**One liner (from challenge paper):** Help ensure equitable and effective cancer care for everyone in Finland by identifying where pathways need strengthening (earlier detection, smoother referrals, faster treatment initiation); develop nationwide AI-powered solution.

## Mandatory requirements
- Identify evidence-based differences in cancer care access or treatment outcomes.
- Locate the stage: detection, transition primary care -> specialist, diagnosis, or time-to-treatment.
- Recommend specific evidence-based improvement(s) that national or wellbeing-services-county actors could implement.
- Demonstrate a working solution (slides alone fail).
- Use reliable public aggregate data and/or documented mentor insight; cite sources.
- No personal/patient-level data provided or required.
- Communicate the finding and action within two minutes to a non-technical decision-maker.

## Official AZ weights
- Actionability 30%; Insight 25%; Data quality & transparency 20%; Execution 15%; Clarity 10%.

## Opportunity spaces to explore, NOT assumed solutions
- Screening invitation -> participation; positive test -> follow-up; symptomatic contact -> specialist referral; diagnosis -> treatment.
- Regional, socioeconomic, language, age, service capacity or access differences where data supports comparison.
- How counties interpret evidence and decide which interventions to implement.

## Evidence discipline
All source statements need URL, publisher, date, population, geography, measure definition, numerator/denominator where feasible, limitations. Never imply cross-region correlation proves the cause. Beware year mismatches: 2025 preliminary colorectal screening is not the same full-follow-up dataset as 2024 annual.
